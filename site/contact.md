# Contact

SimpleX: `{{contact.simplex}}`. We answer there.

Mail: `contact@sigelo.io` receives only, so include your SimpleX address.

For a Pro invoice (a month or a year) or an Enterprise quote, send over SimpleX your operator identity (`sigelo-agent whoami`) and your business name, address and VAT number.

Nobody from the project will ever ask for a seed, an identity or wallet private key, or a token. A payment address comes only with an invoice you asked for, in the SimpleX conversation you opened with the address on `https://sigelo.io/contact.html`.

How to pay is published only here and in `site/contact.md` on the main branch of `github.com/csigelo/sigelo-relay`.
